Discover The History Of Columbus City

The city of Columbus, Ohio was born on February 14th, 1812, and has become the most populated city in the state. The Scioto and Cuyahoga rivers flow through it and converge through the city. They were used to help build up the early state's infrastructure through the Lake Erie Channel. The center capital became the government's meeting sight. Preserved historic buildings and museums still present important documents for viewing.
